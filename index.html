Our Wedding Journey

Three days of celebration, three milestones of love. Join us as we mark the most important moments of our lives together.

July 3rd
Dholki
A lively pre-wedding celebration filled with music, dance, and traditional festivities. Join us for an evening of joy and anticipation as we celebrate with our closest loved ones.
July 4th
Nikkah
The sacred marriage ceremony where we formally enter into marriage. This beautiful and meaningful moment will unite us in the eyes of our faith and family.
July 5th
Shaalima Reception
A celebratory reception to share an evening of fine dining, dancing, and endless joy with all of our cherished guests as we celebrate our union.
